# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-03

First public release.

- Sync Instagram saved posts and collections into a local library.
- Filter by author, collection and type. Batch cleanup that syncs back to Instagram.
- Video player with keyboard controls, carousels, and fresh links when old ones expire.
- AI report on any post from its frames and audio, exported as Markdown.
- Chrome extension published on the Chrome Web Store; one-line installers for Mac and Windows.
- Open any Instagram account and analyze its posts.
- Instagram safety: one call at a time, at most 150 per hour, automatic pauses when Instagram refuses.
